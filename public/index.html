Adaptive strategy recommendations

ULTRON ranks owner-approved lessons using measured, independently verified outcomes. A promising lesson needs at least five terminal observations and 80% verifier-passed operational success before you can approve it as the preferred strategy for that matching mission type. Approval changes future missions only; existing missions keep their saved strategy. You can clear the preference at any time. ULTRON never changes strategy without your approval.

Tracks only missions that actually used an approved preferred strategy. Each attempt is recorded once, including retries and independent-verifier rejections.
